How the podcast content will be stored?

Podcast content is primarily stored on a dedicated podcast hosting platform, which acts as the central server for your audio files. Unlike social media videos, you cannot upload directly to apps like Spotify or Apple Podcasts; instead, these apps "read" your content from the host via an RSS feed.
