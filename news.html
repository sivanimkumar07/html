City residents,School students join hands to help kerala flood victims

Gurugram:City residents are doing their bit to help people in kerala,which is facing its worst flood in nearly a century.People from different parts of the city are coming together to collect items of daily use and basic medicines.Many parts of kerala have been washed out in incessant rains. The death toll in the state has reached 180.Rescue efforts are underway to save stranded people. At least 324 people have lost their lives in rain-related incidents.
